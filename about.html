About me

Who I am, what I’m curious about, and what I do when I’m not working.

My name is Tingxuan. I am interested in exploring ideas around natural science, the brain, intelligence, the computational sciences and anywhere in between.

This site is a small, quiet corner of the internet where I keep track of the ideas I have contemplated, the projects I have done, and the technical skills I have learned, and share them with you.

I have always believed the best way to learn is by teaching, so I hope that we can make progress together through these posts. I think writing a blog here is very nice because it is slower than a tweet, less formal than a paper, and more public than a notebook.

Right now

Especially interested in AI for science, creating scientific data pipelines, and models that can survive contact with messy real-world systems and help us understand or answer scientific questions.

I am also drawn to questions in neuroscience and cognition, and how they might be related to computational methods we have today.
